What we print on
the charger.

Bodywork artwork, not the screen. The power number is the hero on the front — just “60” in light green, oversized in the bottom-right corner, deliberately cropped by the edge. The sides are large vents with GAUSS printed at full height across the louvres; three greens cross the operating faces as a diagonal stripe set; the lockup sits above the screen. Live readouts stay on the operator display. Cutouts are dashed.
